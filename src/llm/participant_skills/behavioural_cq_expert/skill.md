# Behavioural CQ Expert Skill Profile

## Target
- Test culturally calibrated visible behavior: wording, tone, pace, silence, channel, audience, timing, sequence, and follow-through.
- Behavioural CQ is strongest when adaptation must still deliver task performance.
- The best action is not automatically the softest, most deferential, or most polite.

## Score Seed Fit
- Score high when the seed turns on delivery, channel, public/private handling, turn-taking, escalation, repair, or action sequence.
- Score low when the main task is interpreting meaning without visible execution.

## Task A Design
- Prefer concrete actions, exact wording, channel choices, or sequences over abstract evaluation.
- Put newcomer's default behavioral script, seed's local interaction logic, and performance constraint in tension.
- Correct answer should have a visible cost: slower closure, less public clarity, extra preparation, or more relational work.
- Include one option that says the right thing in the wrong channel.
- Include one option that uses the right channel at the wrong time.
- Include one option that is efficient but breaks local rhythm.
- Include one option that over-accommodates and loses ownership or clarity.
- Sometimes the calibrated behavior is active or direct; do not reward softness when the local frame expects timely contribution.

## Task B Design
- The newcomer should do several things well; the decisive issue should be a subtle mismatch in timing, audience, channel, rhythm, or close-of-meeting ownership.
- Local cues should require behavioral adjustment, not simply state the norm.
- Appropriate dialogues may include repair and compromise, not perfect behavior.
- The decisive behavior should be a culture-specific execution detail, not a universal polite script.
- Make speed, directness, transparency, privacy, patience, and deference label-neutral until the target knowledge decides them.
- Avoid stock surface cues such as "follow your lead" for appropriate or "No need / skip / copy everyone" for inappropriate.
- Use mixed execution: include one well-calibrated behavior and one questionable behavior in both labels.
- Do not make the final turn a clean fix or a repeated wrong plan; the label should rest on a subtle form, intensity, sequence, audience, or follow-up distinction.

## Distractor Standard
- Wrong answers should share the right intention but fail in form, intensity, sequence, audience, or follow-up.
