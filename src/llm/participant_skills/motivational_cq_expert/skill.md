# Motivational CQ Expert Skill Profile

## Target
- Test willingness to enter, sustain, repair, and adapt within costly cross-cultural situations.
- Motivational CQ is not friendliness. It includes intrinsic interest, self-efficacy, persistence, and disciplined engagement under stress.
- The answer should show adaptive effort with boundaries, not blind persistence.

## Score Seed Fit
- Score high when progress is slow, ambiguous, socially costly, tiring, or relationship-dependent.
- Score low when the item mainly tests knowledge, calibration, or visible action.

## Task A Design
- Prefer concrete choices: what effort to invest, what follow-up to own, what inconvenience to absorb, when to pause, and how to keep engagement productive.
- Put local relationship logic, newcomer's efficiency/status expectation, and real operational cost in tension.
- Correct answer should require visible cost while avoiding unproductive pressure.
- Include one energetic but intrusive distractor.
- Include one respectful but disengaged distractor.
- Include one patient-sounding distractor that shifts adaptation burden to the local side.
- Include one value-aligned or inclusive-sounding option that lacks resources, follow-through, or learning effort.
- Sometimes high motivation means taking ownership or pushing a bounded next step, not waiting for the relationship to become comfortable.

## Task B Design
- Judge sustained engagement across turns: listening, repair, follow-up ownership, adaptation cost, and task movement.
- The newcomer may sound warm but still withdraw; or persist visibly while ignoring the local form of relationship work.
- Do not let friendly tone decide the label.
- Make the effort culturally targeted: more time, warmth, persistence, or deference is not automatically appropriate.
- Inappropriate dialogues should be good-faith and partly adaptive, with the failure in where effort is invested or which burden is shifted.
- Appropriate dialogues may preserve speed, directness, or task closure when the target knowledge calls for it.
- Both labels should include real effort; the difference is whether that effort is culturally placed, not whether the newcomer tries.
- Avoid final-turn summaries that make persistence obviously right or wrong.

## Distractor Standard
- Wrong answers should be plausible forms of avoidance, over-insistence, performative openness, low-cost tolerance, or self-efficacy overreach.
